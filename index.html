    Vantage (Voluntary Advising Network: Talent → Advice → Growth → Employment) is a Griz Career Project Lab prototype — Team: Ben Stanton, Hunter Holms, Preston Baker. The 30 mentors shown are illustrative placeholders (not real people) standing in for the real UM Mentor Program roster this would launch with. Applying, picking preferred mentors, and the mentor sign-up are mocked up to show how the flow would work; a real deployment routes every application to a program coordinator, who makes the final match — preferred mentors are a signal, never a guarantee. Your applications and mentor sign-ups are saved so our team can see what's actually being used.
